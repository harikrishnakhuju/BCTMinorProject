\chapter*{Abstract}
        The Facial Recognition Attendance System is an advanced solution designed to
 streamline and enhance attendance tracking processes. This system accurately identifies and records individuals' attendance in real-time. The technology's feasibility is evaluated across technical, economic, operational, legal, and ethical dimensions, highlighting its potential to revolutionize 
traditional attendance tracking methods.The traditional face recognition technology is more complicated for the extraction of facial image features and the selection of classifiers, and the recognition rate is not high. With the continuous maturity of the convolutional neural network from handwritten
 digit recognition to face recognition, A face recognition algorithm that tests CNN using the Python+OpenCV framework. This model can automatically extract facial image features and classify them. Using adam optimizer and Harr Cascade classifier
 for face recognition can make training faster convergence and more.
